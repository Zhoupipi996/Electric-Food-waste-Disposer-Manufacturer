Capabilities › Quality Control

Structured quality control, on every order

Four inspection stages run on every order, whether it's a 500-unit ready-inventory purchase or a full ODM development project.

  • Incoming Material Inspection — components and raw materials checked against spec before production begins.
  • In-Process Inspection — line checks during assembly to catch defects before they compound.
  • Final Product Inspection — completed units tested against full functional and cosmetic spec.
  • Pre-Shipment Random Sampling — statistical sampling from the finished batch before it leaves the factory.
