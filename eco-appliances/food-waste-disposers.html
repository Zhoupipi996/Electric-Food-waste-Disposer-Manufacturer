FAQ

What is an electric food waste disposer?

An electric food waste disposer — also called an electric food cycler or electric kitchen composter — is a countertop appliance that grinds, dries, and shrinks food scraps into a small volume of dry residue, typically reducing waste volume by up to 96%, using a BLDC motor and a heated or ventilated drying cycle.

What's the difference between an electric food cycler and a garbage disposal?

A traditional garbage disposal grinds waste and flushes it down the drain using running water. An electric food cycler / electric kitchen composter instead dries and shrinks food waste inside a sealed bucket, producing a dry, low-volume output with no plumbing connection required.

What is the minimum order quantity for OEM electric kitchen composters?

Standard MOQ for BESTAR's Eco Appliances line is 500 units. Sample and light-customization runs may be available in smaller quantities — ask our team directly.
