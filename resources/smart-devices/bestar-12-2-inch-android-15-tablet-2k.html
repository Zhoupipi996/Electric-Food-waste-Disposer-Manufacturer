BESTAR Introduces the 12.2-Inch Android Tablet with 2.4K Display and 120Hz Refresh

Published December 2025 · BESTAR Team

BESTAR's 12.2″ Android Tablet pairs a metal housing with a 2.4K, 120Hz IPS display — a spec combination built for creative professionals, designers, and B2B software integrators who need genuine visual precision on an OEM Android tablet PC.

Design and build

The metal housing cover improves heat dissipation and gives the tablet a premium, industrial finish suited to long-duration use in education, enterprise mobility, or workstation environments. The 2400×1600 IPS panel at 120Hz delivers smooth scrolling, video playback, and stylus-writing responsiveness that a 60Hz panel can't match.

Platform

Under the hood: an Octa-core processor, 8GB RAM, 128GB storage, and optional gyroscope and geomagnetic sensor (42607 + MMC5603) for spatial orientation, AR interaction, and precision navigation — alongside original handwriting-pen support and dual-SIM GSM connectivity for field use where WiFi is limited.

Where it fits

This spec sheet spans a wide range of B2B use cases: interactive classrooms and stylus-based assignments in education; HMI and industrial control dashboards; enterprise mobility for field technicians and inspectors; retail and POS ordering systems; and hospitality room-control panels. The pen support in particular opens up digital sketching, patient-data annotation, and signature capture use cases that a standard touch-only tablet can't serve as well.

Customizing this platform

As with the rest of BESTAR's Tablet PC range, this model is available through Light OEM branding or full ODM development if your project needs a different memory configuration, sensor combination, or enclosure.
