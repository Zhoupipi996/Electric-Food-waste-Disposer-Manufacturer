BESTAR Introduces the Mountable Digital Calendar with Full HD Touchscreen

Published December 2025 · BESTAR Team

A wall-mountable digital photo frame and calendar line, available in 10.1″ and 15.6″ touchscreen sizes, built for family photo sharing, smart digital calendars, and OEM/ODM branding on top of the same wall-mounted digital display platform used across BESTAR's Interactive Digital Signage range.

What it does

Cloud photo album syncing, family photo and video sharing, and a smart digital calendar with reminders and notifications sit alongside multiple display modes — slideshow, dashboard, agenda view — with automatic brightness and scheduling. A G-sensor supports auto-rotate, and high-resolution 2K/2.4K IPS panel options keep photo and video output vibrant.

Smart-home integration

Beyond calendar and photo functionality, the platform supports smart-home control dashboards, media playback and streaming apps, optional voice-assistant compatibility, kiosk/app-lock mode for dedicated single-purpose use, and OTA firmware updates for remote fleet management — the same underlying capabilities as BESTAR's wall-mounted digital display line.

OEM/ODM customization

Custom UI/UX for a digital-frame or digital-calendar use case, logo and boot-animation branding, pre-installed apps and cloud platforms, multiple size options for home vs. commercial deployment, and FCC/CE/RoHS certification support are all available — the same OEM/ODM model BESTAR applies across its Smart Devices catalog.
