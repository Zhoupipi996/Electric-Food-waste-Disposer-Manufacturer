Private Label & ODM Electric Food Cycler MOQ Guide

April 2026 · BESTAR Team

Two different questions get conflated when brands ask about MOQ for an electric food cycler program: "what's the minimum to place an order" and "what's the minimum to get a custom design built." The answers are very different.

Tier 1–2: ordering an existing model

For a Ready Inventory purchase or Light OEM branding on an existing electric food waste disposer model, BESTAR's standard MOQ is 500 units. This covers your branding, packaging, and any firmware-level customization on the existing platform — no new tooling required, so lead time is primarily production scheduling, not engineering.

Tier 3: full ODM development

A genuinely custom electric kitchen composter — different bucket capacity, a proprietary motor spec, or a housing shape outside the existing range — requires new mold tooling. This is a materially different commitment: higher upfront tooling cost, an NNN agreement before design work begins, and a longer timeline running through concept, engineering validation, and pilot production before mass production starts.

Sample lead times

For an existing-model OEM sample, expect a shorter turnaround since no new parts need to be sourced or molded. For a full ODM sample, lead time depends heavily on whether the design needs new tooling versus reusing existing mechanical components with a new housing — ask our team for a concrete date range once your spec is defined, rather than assuming a fixed number.

Choosing the right tier for your launch

Most brands entering the category for the first time are better served starting at Tier 1–2 to validate demand on an existing, already-certified platform, then moving to Tier 3 ODM once volume justifies the tooling investment. See BESTAR's Solutions page for the full four-tier model.
