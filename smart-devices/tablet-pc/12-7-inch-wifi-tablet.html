Overview

A fixed-location commercial tablet running Android 14 on a Rockchip Cortex-A72+A53 CPU with a Mali-G52 MC3 GPU, built for multimedia learning platforms, digital signage, and enterprise dashboards where cellular connectivity isn't required. The 2K Incell multi-touch display (1600×2176), 8GB RAM, and 256GB storage support demanding, data-heavy applications, while 5G WiFi and PD fast charging keep it deployment-ready. Available as Light OEM or full ODM.

  • 12.7-inch 2K Incell multi-touch display
  • Rockchip Cortex-A72+A53, Mali-G52 MC3 GPU
  • 8GB RAM / 256GB storage
  • 5G WiFi, Bluetooth 5.0
  • Metal housing cover
