Overview

BESTAR TEK's largest standard Android tablet, built for interactive classrooms and online learning management. An Octa-core 2.0GHz processor, 6GB RAM, and 128GB storage support multimedia learning and content creation, while a front AR camera enables AI-based face recognition for smart attendance and interactive lessons. The 15.6-inch FHD IPS display, 5G WiFi, and Bluetooth 4.2 complete an education-focused platform, open to Light OEM branding or full ODM development.

  • 5G WiFi, Bluetooth 4.2
