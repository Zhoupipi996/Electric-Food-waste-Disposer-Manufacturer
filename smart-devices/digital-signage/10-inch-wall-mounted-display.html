10-Inch Wall-Mounted Digital Display with NFC OEM

MODEL BST-101SH

Send Inquiry Start OEM/ODM Project

Overview

A 10.1-inch wall-mounted Android 11 display for smart-home automation and building control, with a 1920×1200 IPS touchscreen and RJ45+PoE for single-cable power and data. NFC and RFID support enable tap-based access control and meeting-room booking workflows on top of standard signage duties, and the unit includes a built-in Queue Management System for banks, hotels, and retail. Light OEM or full ODM customization available.

  • 10.1-inch 1920x1200 IPS touchscreen
  • RJ45 + PoE, single-cable install
  • NFC/RFID for access control and QMS
  • RS485 and Type-C ports for integration
  • Light OEM or full ODM available
