Four ways to source hardware from BESTAR

Every buyer starts at a different point — testing a new market, launching a lightly branded line, or committing to a fully custom platform. BESTAR's model is built around where you actually are, not a single one-size quote.

1

Ready Inventory & Small-Batch Purchase

The lowest-friction way to start working with BESTAR — no tooling, no MOQ negotiation, no waiting on a custom development schedule.

Check Ready Inventory & Get Sample Pricing
  • Ready inventory across current Tablet PC, E-ink Tablet, and Food Waste Disposer models
  • Small-batch order quantities, no custom tooling required
  • Fastest available lead time — ships from existing stock or short production runs
  • Ideal for market testing, trade-show samples, or first-time buyers evaluating BESTAR
2

Light OEM Customization

Put your brand on a proven, already-certified model without the cost or timeline of ground-up development.

Request Light Customization Quote
  • Logo application, custom packaging, and color/material options on existing tooling
  • Underlying certifications carry over — no separate certification project required
  • Sample turnaround typically faster than full ODM, since tooling already exists
  • Ideal for brand owners who want differentiation without a new mold
3

Full ODM/OEM Development

Ground-up custom engineering — new industrial design, new tooling, or new functional specification — once your order volume reaches development-justifying minimums.

Start a Custom Development Project
  • Custom industrial design, structural engineering, and new mold development
  • Dedicated engineering review at each stage — concept, prototype, pre-production, mass production
  • Full certification project management for your target markets
  • Ideal for brand owners with a defined product vision and committed order volume
IP protection: Full ODM/OEM projects are covered by an NNN agreement (Non-Use, Non-Disclosure, Non-Circumvention) before any design work begins — the structure commonly used for China-based manufacturing to protect a buyer's design and market access, not just confidentiality.
4

Display & System Integration

Smart Devices Division only. For buyers deploying tablets or displays as part of a larger system rather than a standalone retail product.

Discuss Your Integration Requirements
  • Mounting, enclosure, and kiosk-integration engineering support
  • Custom firmware/UI configuration for signage, POS, or self-service deployments
  • Coordination with your own hardware or software integrators where applicable
  • Ideal for systems integrators, kiosk builders, and digital signage deployers
