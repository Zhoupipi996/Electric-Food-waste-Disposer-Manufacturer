FAQ

Common questions from software & appliance partners

If your question isn't covered here, send it through the contact form and we'll get back to you directly.

What's the difference between the four tiers?

Tier 1 is a ready-inventory purchase with no customization. Tier 2 is light OEM branding on an existing model. Tier 3 is full ODM/OEM development from a new specification, protected by an NNN agreement. Tier 4 (Smart Devices only) is display/system integration support. See the Solutions page for details on each.

What is your minimum order quantity (MOQ)?

Our standard MOQ is 500 units. Tier 1 ready-inventory purchases can sometimes go lower — ask us directly if 500 doesn't fit your project.

What certifications do you carry?

Smart Devices products carry ISO 9001, ISO 14001, CE, FCC, TELEC, EMC, LVD, UN38.3, and MSDS documentation. Eco Appliances products carry PSE, ETL/UL, KC, CE, ERP, CB, and TÜV. Certification testing runs alongside development, and we can provide certificate documentation for your specific order.

What is an NNN agreement and do I need one?

NNN stands for Non-Use, Non-Disclosure, Non-Circumvention — a contract structure commonly used for China-based manufacturing to protect your design and market access, not just confidentiality. We put one in place before any Tier 3 full ODM/OEM design work begins.

Which platforms do your tablets run on?

Our Smart Devices line is built on MediaTek, Rockchip, Spreadtrum, and Allwinner platforms, covering 7″ to 15.6″ screen sizes.

Where do you ship?

We ship by air, sea, and express freight into Europe, the US, Japan, and Korea — our Eco Appliances line carries pre-built certification for all four markets, and our Smart Devices line has a track record across the same regions.

Can you develop a fully custom device from a spec?

Yes — that's the Tier 3 full ODM/OEM path. Send your requirement through the contact form and we'll follow up with a feasibility read within one business day.
